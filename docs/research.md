# Creative research — Signal Atlas

Research conducted on 7 October 2026, before application implementation. Started at [Codrops All Posts](https://tympanus.net/codrops/all-posts/), followed [page 2](https://tympanus.net/codrops/all-posts/page/2/) and [page 3](https://tympanus.net/codrops/all-posts/page/3/), then read the 16 linked articles below individually. Every linked live target was opened or attempted.

Evidence key: **Article** means the author describes the effect or architecture; **Live HTML** means the opened page exposed the stated content or controls; **Visual** means a rendered browser screenshot was inspected. Web text alone cannot verify motion, frame rate, typography appearance, or responsive behavior. “Not established” means the article and available live evidence do not justify a claim. No source code, visual assets, or exact layouts were copied.

## Five patterns to transfer

1. **One interaction that explains the work.** Cerebrium makes infrastructure tangible and Dash connects motion to its positioning. For Kush, an original SVG vector manifold can communicate retrieval and connected reasoning. Hover, touch, and keyboard selection should reveal the same labeled data points. The interaction is a small explanatory instrument beside a clear introduction and resume link. It must never delay reading.
2. **Editorial hierarchy with technical annotations.** HAOQI combines emphatic display text with compact system details; PX PUSH gives the whole experience one coherent organizing concept. Signal Atlas can use generous sans-serif headings, occasional italic serif emphasis, small numbered captions, restrained rules, and ivory/plum themes. This supports a research profile while making projects and education immediately scannable.
3. **An index first, evidence second.** HAOQI preserves CSS/DOM project structure; the GSAP gallery connects a thumbnail to its detail view. Our project index should show title, category, short outcome, and stack before opening a fuller problem/approach/result narrative. Case-study routes remain ordinary links with their own metadata, and factual results are labeled by their source.
4. **One motion vocabulary, one scroll clock.** HAOQI and PX PUSH describe synchronization problems solved by one animation heartbeat. Use consistent short reveals and gentle transitions, integrating Lenis with the chosen animation library deliberately. Preserve native mobile scroll; avoid infinite scrolling, long pins, and animations that make a recruiter wait for content.
5. **Budgets and fallbacks are design decisions.** Yichen, ASCII Logo, Xylophone, ULTRAGRID, and FRONTIER all expose concrete tradeoffs. Our lower-cost SVG signature should reserve dimensions, stop unnecessary work out of view, respond to reduced motion, and retain useful content without animation. Themes need intentional contrast and visible focus. No heavyweight 3D is justified merely by the brief’s optional allowance.

These are design inferences, not claims about Kush’s achievements. The subject-specific application is grounded in the separately reviewed resume/GitHub content: RAG, vision, multi-agent systems, a 12-band crop-disease transformer, SLA monitoring, and ensemble prediction.

## Implementation implications

Signal Atlas is an original synthesis: an editorial research portfolio with an SVG retrieval manifold, a concise finite project index, and ordinary case-study pages. Use two fonts at most. Keep all primary text in semantic HTML, reserve visual dimensions, show sources for factual outcomes, and keep resume/contact paths visible. Motion should support orientation rather than create delays. Reduced motion, keyboard focus, small phones, and both themes must receive direct QA. Lighthouse results belong in validation documentation after measurement; none of the source sites’ scores are evidence for our implementation.
